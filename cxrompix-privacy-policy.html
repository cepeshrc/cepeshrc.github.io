Privacy Policy for Cxrompix

Effective & Last Updated: November 2026 • Architecture: Manifest V3 (100% Local Processing)

Zero-Telemetry Guarantee: Cxrompix processes all image detection, 4K resolution upgrades, PNG/JPG conversions, and ZIP archiving strictly locally inside your browser. We never collect, store, or sell your browsing history or downloaded media.

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

This Privacy Policy governs how the "Cxrompix — Pro Image Downloader & 4K Media Extractor" browser extension ("we," "our," or "the extension") handles information. Engineered with a strict Client-Side First architecture, all core media inspection, format conversion, and batch downloading operations execute locally on your device without transmitting personal data or visited URLs to our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Cxrompix is built for complete privacy by design. We do NOT collect, log, store, or transmit:

  • Your browsing history, visited URLs, search queries, or active tab content.
  • The images, videos, filenames, or ZIP archives you preview, convert, or save.
  • Personal identifiers, cookies, authentication credentials, or web form inputs.
  • Third-party analytics telemetry, behavioral tracking pixels, or injected advertisements.

3. Local Page Interaction, Conversion & Extraction

To deliver instant media extraction when enabled by you, Cxrompix interacts locally with webpage Document Object Models (DOM) and browser rendering APIs:

  • Media & Live Radar Detection: Scans the active page locally to identify standard media nodes (<img>, <picture>, <video>), high-resolution srcset candidates, and CSS background assets, Piercing transparent overlay wrappers where necessary.
  • On-Page Interactive Controls: Temporarily renders lightweight UI elements (such as the Cursor tracker, 0.3s Hold ring, Hover download button, or Batch selection toolbar) inside your active tab.
  • In-Memory Conversion & Packaging: Uses hardware-accelerated browser OffscreenCanvas to convert WebP/AVIF images into clean PNG or JPG files and packages multi-file selections into a local ZIP archive directly in your device's RAM.

No third-party proxy servers are used to fetch or convert your media. Every operation occurs directly between your browser and the website hosting the image.

4. Local Device Storage Only

Cxrompix utilizes Chrome's built-in storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local) strictly to persist your configuration on your own browser profile, including:

  • Engine activation state, Popup vs. Side Panel layout preference, Light/Dark theme, and Tab-Only Scope settings.
  • Selected capture trigger (1-Click, Alt + Click, or Hold 0.3s) and Cursor/Hover mode.
  • Anti-WebP output preferences, 4K Original Hunter toggle, minimum pixel filters, custom subfolder paths, and file naming templates.
  • Local daily Free-tier usage counters, which automatically reset at midnight (00:00) on your device.

6. Transparent Permissions Justification

Cxrompix requests only the minimum browser permissions required to execute user-initiated media downloads:

  • storage: Saves your interface preferences, naming templates, filters, and local daily quota state across browser sessions.
  • downloads: Saves single images, converted PNG/JPG files, and compiled ZIP archives to your computer, routes files to user-defined subfolders, and detects local format compatibility (such as WebP/AVIF downloads) without uploading any file data.
  • declarativeNetRequest: Attaches standard origin-matching Referer and Origin headers locally on outbound media requests to hotlink-protected CDNs (such as pinimg.com, cdninstagram.com, fbcdn.net, and twimg.com) so high-resolution downloads do not fail with HTTP 403 errors—eliminating any need for external proxy servers.
  • tabs: Communicates with the active tab to run the Live Media Radar, enforce "Active on This Tab Only" isolation, and read the page's domain hostname when applying the {domain} file naming variable.
  • sidePanel: Allows you to dock the Cxrompix interface inside Chrome's native Side Panel when toggled.
  • alarms: Schedules lightweight local timers in the Manifest V3 service worker to reset daily Free-tier counters at midnight and manage optional local notifications.
  • notifications: Displays native system status alerts when large batch downloads or ZIP archives finish processing.
  • Host Permissions (http://*/*, https://*/*, https://extensionpay.com/*): Required to detect and fetch user-selected media across websites and CDNs for local conversion/archiving, and to verify PLATINUM license status via ExtensionPay.

7. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

Cxrompix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including all Limited Use requirements. User data is never sold, transferred to data brokers, or used for creditworthiness, lending, or personalized advertising.

8. Your Control & Data Deletion

You retain full control over all extension settings. You can reset preferences at any time inside the Core Settings page, or uninstall Cxrompix from your browser to immediately and permanently erase all locally stored extension data from your device.
